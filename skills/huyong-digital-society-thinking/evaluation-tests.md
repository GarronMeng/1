# Evaluation Tests

This file helps evaluate whether an AI assistant is actually using the Hu Yong Digital Society Thinking Skill.

Use these tests after installing or editing the skill.

---

## Test 1: AI Writing

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式数字社会分析框架，分析 AI 写作为什么不只是效率工具。
```

### Strong answer should include

- Writing as thinking vs writing as output optimization.
- Human judgment and subjectivity.
- Machine fluency as a new standard.
- Delegated thinking or outsourced discomfort.
- Not just pros and cons.

### Weak answer signs

- Only says AI writing saves time.
- Only lists advantages and disadvantages.
- Does not discuss how writing changes as a human practice.

## Test 2: Xiaohongshu Medical Content

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析小红书医疗科普内容。
```

### Strong answer should include

- Xiaohongshu as a trust-production system.
- Personal experience, expert authority, algorithmic visibility, and commercial conversion.
- Anxiety amplification risk.
- The difference between patient story and medical evidence.
- The question of whether users become more informed or more anxious.

### Weak answer signs

- Only discusses traffic growth.
- Only gives content marketing tips.
- Treats the platform as a neutral channel.

---

## Test 3: Hospital Digitalization

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析医院数字化转型。
```

### Strong answer should include

- Digitalization as reorganization of patient, doctor, hospital, insurer, and data systems.
- Trust journey rather than only online appointment.
- Data visibility and institutional responsibility.
- Patient agency and opacity.
- Strategic implication for hospital communication and service design.

### Weak answer signs

- Only says online appointment improves efficiency.
- Only mentions electronic medical records.
- Does not discuss trust or patient agency.

## Test 4: Short Video Platforms

### Prompt

```text
为什么短视频平台不只是娱乐平台？用胡泳式数字社会视角分析。
```

### Strong answer should include

- Short video as attention allocation system.
- Algorithmic visibility.
- Public discussion vs emotional circulation.
- Platform incentives shaping creators and users.
- The kind of perception, patience, and public life cultivated by the platform.

### Weak answer signs

- Only discusses addiction.
- Only criticizes low-quality content.
- Does not analyze platform architecture or incentives.

## Test 5: AI Agent

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析 AI Agent 为什么不只是自动化工具。
```

### Strong answer should include

- AI agents as delegation infrastructure.
- Reallocation of judgment and responsibility.
- Human supervision, dependency, and opacity.
- Workflow redesign.
- The question of whether humans become more capable or merely more dependent.

### Weak answer signs

- Only says AI agents automate tasks.
- Only lists use cases.
- Does not discuss delegated judgment.

---

## Test 6: Public Discussion

### Prompt

```text
为什么社交媒体上人人都能发声，但公共讨论反而更难？用胡泳式框架分析。
```

### Strong answer should include

- Expression is not the same as deliberation.
- More voices do not automatically create better public discussion.
- Attention competition and emotional amplification.
- Platform incentives.
- Listening, interpretation, and public reason.

### Weak answer signs

- Only blames users for being emotional.
- Only says information overload.
- Does not discuss platform-mediated visibility.

## General Scoring Rubric

Score each output from 0 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No digital society analysis. Generic answer. |
| 1 | Mentions technology impact but stays at surface level. |
| 2 | Identifies some social effects but lacks structure. |
| 3 | Includes displacement and relationship analysis. |
| 4 | Adds power, visibility, trust, or agency analysis. |
| 5 | Produces a layered argument with human subjectivity and actionable implication. |

---

## Minimum Passing Standard

A passing answer should include at least four of the following:

- surface phenomenon;
- deeper displacement;
- changed relationships;
- governing mechanism;
- power or visibility structure;
- trust mechanism;
- human subjectivity question;
- strategic, cultural, or ethical implication.
