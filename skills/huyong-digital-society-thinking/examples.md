# Examples

This file provides example prompts and expected analytical directions for the Hu Yong Digital Society Thinking Skill.

---

## 1. AI Writing

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析 AI 写作。
```

### Expected direction

- Do not only discuss productivity.
- Analyze how AI changes the relationship between writing and thinking.
- Ask whether AI enhances or weakens human judgment.
- Discuss the risk that human expression becomes standardized by machine fluency.

### Sample answer direction

AI writing should not be understood only as a productivity tool. Its deeper significance is that it changes the relationship between people and expression.

The displacement is from writing as a process of thinking to writing as an optimized output. This does not mean AI writing is bad. But it means the key question has changed: do people still use writing to clarify their own judgment, or do they increasingly use AI to bypass the discomfort of thinking?

## 2. Short Video Platforms

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析短视频平台。
```

### Expected direction

- Treat short video as an attention allocation system.
- Analyze how platforms format behavior.
- Distinguish expression, attention, public discussion, and truth.
- Ask what kind of person the platform encourages users to become.

### Sample answer direction

Short video platforms are not merely entertainment tools. They are attention allocation systems.

The deeper displacement is from content seeking audiences to algorithms assigning audiences to content. The platform does not simply display what people like. It also trains what people become likely to like.

## 3. Hospital Digitalization

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析医院数字化转型。
```

### Expected direction

- Do not only discuss online appointment or operational efficiency.
- Analyze how digital systems reorganize doctor-patient relationships.
- Discuss patient visibility, data, trust, insurance, and institutional responsibility.
- Ask whether digitalization expands patient agency or creates new opacity.

### Sample answer direction

Hospital digitalization should not be reduced to online appointment systems, electronic medical records, or operational dashboards.

Its deeper meaning is the reorganization of the relationship between patients, doctors, hospitals, insurers, platforms, and data systems. The key question is not only whether the hospital becomes more efficient, but whether the patient becomes more capable of understanding, choosing, and participating in care.

## 4. Xiaohongshu Medical Content

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析小红书医疗科普内容。
```

### Expected direction

- Treat Xiaohongshu as a trust-production system, not just a content platform.
- Analyze the relationship between personal experience, professional authority, platform recommendation, and commercial conversion.
- Ask whether patient stories create understanding or anxiety-driven consumption.

### Sample answer direction

Xiaohongshu medical content is not only health education. It is a trust-production mechanism.

Its power comes from the mixture of personal experience, algorithmic visibility, emotional resonance, and commercial decision-making. The question is not simply whether the content is popular, but what kind of trust it produces and whether that trust helps users understand medicine or accelerates anxiety-driven decisions.

## 5. AI Agent Tools

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式数字社会框架分析 AI Agent 为什么不只是效率工具。
```

### Expected direction

- Analyze AI agents as delegation infrastructure.
- Ask what kinds of judgment are being outsourced.
- Identify dependency, opacity, and agency issues.
- Discuss how AI agents reshape work routines and decision authority.

### Sample answer direction

AI agents are not only automation tools. They are delegation infrastructures.

The deeper displacement is from humans operating tools directly to humans defining goals while machines execute, search, summarize, decide, and coordinate across systems. This changes the meaning of work: people are not simply doing less; they are being asked to decide what can be delegated, what must be supervised, and what should remain human judgment.

## 6. Public Discussion and Social Media

### Prompt

```text
用胡泳式框架分析为什么社交媒体上人人都能发声，但公共讨论反而更困难。
```

### Expected direction

- Distinguish voice from public discussion.
- Analyze attention competition and emotional amplification.
- Discuss platform incentives and fragmented publics.
- Ask whether systems support listening and deliberation, not only expression.

### Sample answer direction

The internet lowered the threshold for expression, but expression is not the same as public discussion.

More voices can produce more visibility, but not necessarily more understanding. When platform incentives reward speed, emotion, conflict, and identity performance, public discussion can be transformed into attention competition.

The key issue is whether a system helps people listen, interpret, and deliberate, or only helps them speak louder.
