## 9. Citation Discipline

When writing with this skill, label claims as:

- **Direct source claim**: explicitly supported by a source.
- **Paraphrase**: close restatement of source meaning.
- **Interpretation**: an analytical reading of the source.
- **Extension**: applying the framework to a new case.
- **Original application**: new analysis not claimed as Hu Yong's argument.

Recommended wording:

> 受胡泳关于数字社会、公共讨论与人的主体性的研究关切启发，可以把这个问题理解为……

Avoid:

> 胡泳说……

unless a precise source and exact quotation are provided.
