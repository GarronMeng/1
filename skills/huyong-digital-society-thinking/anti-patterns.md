# Anti-Patterns

This file lists common failure modes. Use it to revise outputs that look intelligent but lack depth.

---

## 1. Consulting Report Syndrome

### Symptom

The answer is organized like a deck:

- background;
- problem;
- analysis;
- recommendation;
- action plan.

### Why it fails

It may be useful, but it does not produce public-intellectual insight. It turns a social condition into a management problem too quickly.

### How to fix

Before giving recommendations, deepen the problem:

- What older order is being displaced?
- What apparatus mediates the new order?
- What paradox does the technology produce?
- What kind of human subject is being shaped?

---

## 2. Platform-as-Channel Error

### Symptom

The answer treats platforms as neutral distribution channels.

Example:

> 小红书帮助医生传播知识，用户可以更方便地获取信息。

### Why it fails

A platform is not merely a channel. It ranks, filters, compares, recommends, monetizes, archives, and repeats. It changes what counts as visible, credible, and worth imitating.

### How to fix

Name the apparatus:

- search;
- recommendation;
- ranking;
- comments;
- likes;
- saves;
- trending topics;
- profile identity;
- metrics;
- moderation;
- commercial incentives.

---

## 3. Pros-and-Cons Trap

### Symptom

The answer says:

- advantage 1;
- advantage 2;
- risk 1;
- risk 2.

### Why it fails

A pros-and-cons list describes effects but rarely explains the underlying transformation.

### How to fix

Replace “advantages and disadvantages” with “promise and paradox”.

Example:

> The promise of the platform is access. The paradox is that access often arrives in a form optimized for anxiety, speed, and comparison.

---

## 4. Empty Subjectivity

### Symptom

The answer repeatedly uses words like “主体性”, “agency”, or “dignity” without showing what happens to actual people.

### Why it fails

Subjectivity is not a slogan. It must be shown through changes in judgment, attention, memory, fear, responsibility, or self-understanding.

### How to fix

Ask:

- What can the person no longer judge?
- What does the person begin to fear?
- What does the person outsource?
- What does the person measure himself against?
- What responsibility becomes unclear?

---

## 5. Over-Attribution

### Symptom

The answer writes as if every point is directly from Hu Yong.

### Why it fails

This skill is an inspired framework, not a direct quotation database.

### How to fix

Use cautious wording:

- “胡泳式视角”
- “受胡泳数字社会问题意识启发”
- “可以借用这一框架理解”
- “这是一种延展性应用，不是直接引文”

---

## 6. No Historical Displacement

### Symptom

The answer analyzes only the current phenomenon.

Example:

> AI 写作提高了效率，也可能造成依赖。

### Why it fails

Without historical contrast, the analysis has no depth. It does not show what changed.

### How to fix

Add the older order:

- writing as thinking → writing as fluent output;
- hospital as institution → platform as pre-diagnostic environment;
- public discussion → attention competition;
- tool operation → goal delegation;
- expert authority → peer narrative plus algorithmic visibility.

---

## 7. No Paradox

### Symptom

The answer has concepts but no tension.

### Why it fails

Digital society writing needs contradiction. Technology is powerful because its promise and danger often come from the same mechanism.

### How to fix

Use paradox pairs:

- access / anxiety;
- participation / capture;
- visibility / exposure;
- personalization / standardization;
- automation / responsibility gap;
- expression / noise;
- connection / isolation;
- fluency / loss of judgment.

---

## 8. Easy Ending

### Symptom

The answer ends with easy advice:

> Therefore hospitals should build professional content and improve transparency.

### Why it fails

Advice may be useful, but public-intellectual writing should not close the question too quickly.

### How to fix

End by clarifying what is at stake:

> The task is not simply to make better content, but to ask whether digital medicine can return proportion, judgment, and responsibility to a patient already surrounded by searchable fear.

---

## 9. Moral Complaint Instead of Social Analysis

### Symptom

The answer blames users, young people, platforms, or technology in general.

### Why it fails

Moral complaint simplifies responsibility. Social analysis shows the apparatus that shapes behavior.

### How to fix

Replace blame with mechanism:

- What is rewarded?
- What is accelerated?
- What is made visible?
- What is made profitable?
- What becomes hard to say or do?

---

## 10. Name-Dropping Without Thinking

### Symptom

The answer mentions “数字社会”, “后真相”, “主体性”, “公共性”, or “平台权力” but does not use them to reveal anything new.

### Why it fails

Concepts should make ordinary experience more intelligible. They should not decorate the paragraph.

### How to fix

For each concept, require a concrete sentence:

- What does it explain?
- What does it make visible?
- What older explanation does it replace?
- What human experience does it illuminate?
