# Output Templates

This file provides reusable output formats for different use cases.

---

## 1. One-Page Analytical Memo

Use this when the user wants a concise but structured analysis.

```markdown
# [Topic]: Digital Society Analysis

## 1. Surface Phenomenon

[Describe what is happening in plain language.]

## 2. Deeper Displacement

The deeper shift is from [old logic] to [new logic].

## 3. Changed Relationships

- [Relationship 1]
- [Relationship 2]
- [Relationship 3]

## 4. Platform / System Power

[Explain who controls visibility, ranking, access, credibility, or decision-making.]

## 5. Human Subjectivity Question

The key question is not only [surface issue], but whether [human agency / judgment / dignity / expression] can remain central.

## 6. Implication

[State the cultural, strategic, institutional, or ethical implication.]
```

---

## 2. Essay Outline

Use this when the user wants a long-form essay or article.

```markdown
# Title

[Sharp title that frames the technology as a social issue.]

## Opening Hook

[Start with a concrete scene, contradiction, or social anxiety.]

## Thesis

The real issue is not whether [technology] is useful, but what kind of [person / institution / relationship / society] it quietly produces.

## Section 1: The Surface Story

[What most people usually say about this topic.]

## Section 2: The Deeper Displacement

[Explain what has shifted structurally.]

## Section 3: The New Relationship Map

[Explain how the technology reorganizes relationships among users, platforms, institutions, experts, publics, and data systems.]

## Section 4: The Power Question

[Who benefits? Who is governed? Who controls visibility and credibility?]

## Section 5: The Human Question

[Discuss judgment, dignity, agency, expression, memory, or responsibility.]

## Ending

[Return to the opening scene and offer a more reflective conclusion.]
```

## 3. Social Media Post

Use this when the user wants a Xiaohongshu, WeChat, LinkedIn, or Twitter-style post.

```markdown
# [Title]

Most people think [technology] is about [common view].

But the deeper issue is this:

> [Technology] is not just a tool. It is changing the relationship between [A], [B], and [C].

## 1. What changed?

[Explain the displacement in simple language.]

## 2. Why does it matter?

[Explain power, visibility, attention, trust, or agency.]

## 3. The real question

The question is not [surface question].

The question is:

> [Human subjectivity question.]

## 4. My takeaway

[One sharp concluding insight.]
```

## 4. Business Strategy Memo

Use this when the topic involves products, platforms, hospitals, brands, or organizational strategy.

```markdown

## What Is Being Reorganized

| Relationship | Old logic | New logic | Strategic implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A-B] | [Old] | [New] | [Implication] |
| [C-D] | [Old] | [New] | [Implication] |
| [E-F] | [Old] | [New] | [Implication] |

## Power and Trust Mechanism

[Explain what creates trust, dependency, visibility, conversion, or institutional authority.]

## Risk

[Explain how the same system may create opacity, anxiety, over-dependence, or loss of agency.]

## Opportunity

[State the actionable opportunity.]

## Recommended Actions

1. [Action 1]
2. [Action 2]
3. [Action 3]
```

## 5. Medical Digitalization Analysis

Use this for hospital, patient, insurance, medical content, online consultation, AI diagnosis, or cross-border healthcare topics.

```markdown
# [Topic]: Medical Digital Society Analysis

## 1. Surface Phenomenon

[Describe the healthcare scenario.]

## 2. Deeper Shift

The shift is from healthcare as a single clinical encounter to healthcare as a digitally mediated trust journey.

## 3. Changed Relationships

- Patient ↔ doctor
- Patient ↔ hospital
- Patient ↔ platform content
- Patient ↔ insurance
- Doctor ↔ data system
- Hospital ↔ public trust

## 4. Trust Infrastructure

[Explain what produces trust: expertise, transparency, patient story, platform visibility, insurance clarity, continuity of care.]

## 5. Risk

[Explain anxiety amplification, misleading content, fragmented responsibility, data opacity, or over-commercialization.]

## 6. Human Question

Does digitalization help patients understand, choose, and participate in care, or does it make them more anxious, more trackable, and more dependent on opaque systems?

## 7. Strategic Implication

[Explain what hospitals, doctors, platforms, or public-interest organizations should do.]
```

## 6. AI Product Critique

Use this for AI tools, AI agents, chatbots, workflow automation, or AI companions.

```markdown
# AI Product Critique: [Product / Scenario]

## 1. What the product claims to do

[State the surface value proposition.]

## 2. What it actually reorganizes

[Explain changed relationships, workflows, decisions, or dependencies.]

## 3. Delegated Judgment

What judgment is being delegated to AI?

- [Judgment 1]
- [Judgment 2]
- [Judgment 3]

## 4. Agency Test

Does the product make users:

- more capable?
- more reflective?
- more dependent?
- more standardized?
- more visible to systems?

## 5. Design Implication

[Suggest how the product should preserve human agency, transparency, and responsibility.]
```

## 7. Title Generator

Use this when the user asks for titles.

Patterns:

- [Technology] is not [common view], but [deeper structure]
- When [technology] becomes [environment / institution / infrastructure]
- The hidden displacement behind [trend]
- Why [more expression / more efficiency / more data] does not mean [better discussion / better judgment / better care]
- The real question about [technology] is not [surface question]

Examples:

- AI写作不是帮你写得更快，而是在重塑你如何思考
- 短视频不是娱乐工具，而是注意力分配制度
- 医院数字化不是线上挂号，而是患者信任旅程的重组
- 人人都能发声之后，为什么公共讨论反而更难了
- AI Agent不是效率工具，而是判断权的重新分配
