## 2. Early Internet and Public Discussion

### 众声喧哗：网络时代的个人表达与公共讨论

Key theme:

- Internet as a space of individual expression and public discussion.
- The tension between voice, participation, noise, and deliberation.

Suggested use:

Use this theme when analyzing social media, online public opinion, platform-mediated speech, or the difference between expression and public discussion.

---

## 3. Digital Displacement

### 数字位移 / Internet displacement themes

Key theme:

- Digital technology does not only improve efficiency; it displaces old centers, boundaries, roles, and institutions.
- Marginal actors, users, platforms, and networks can move toward the center.

Suggested use:

Use this theme when analyzing digital transformation, AI adoption, creator economy, platformization, or institutional disruption.

---

## 4. Platformized Society

### Platform society lectures and interviews

Key theme:

- Platforms should not be treated as neutral channels.
- Platforms operate as infrastructures and institutional arrangements.
- They shape visibility, participation, data flows, and power relations.

Suggested use:

Use this theme when analyzing Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat, YouTube, TikTok, app stores, marketplaces, or AI agent ecosystems.

---

## 5. Digital Divide

### Digital divide discussions

Key theme:

- Digital inequality is not only about access to devices or networks.
- It also concerns usage capacity, social consequences, cultural participation, and unequal exposure to algorithmic systems.

Suggested use:

Use this theme when analyzing education, elderly users, healthcare access, public services, rural-urban gaps, or AI adoption inequality.

---

## 6. AI and Human Subjectivity

### AI-era interviews and essays

Key theme:

- AI should not be analyzed only as a productivity tool.
- The deeper issue is how AI reshapes human judgment, agency, embodied experience, tacit knowledge, creativity, and self-understanding.

Suggested use:

Use this theme when analyzing AI writing, AI companions, AI agents, AI healthcare, education, creative work, and workplace automation.

---

## 7. Citation Discipline

When formal accuracy matters, do not cite this skill as if it were a primary source.

Instead:

1. use this skill to generate analytical angles;
2. locate a relevant primary or reliable secondary source;
3. distinguish direct quotation from paraphrase and interpretation;
4. mark any new application as your own extension.
