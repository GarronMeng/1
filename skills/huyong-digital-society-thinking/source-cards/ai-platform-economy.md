# Source Card: AI and Platform Economy

## Source

财新 interview: “胡泳谈人工智能时代的平台经济”

URL: https://opinion.caixin.com/2024-10-11/102244232.html

## What this source establishes

This source helps ground the platform-economy side of the skill.

It supports the broad idea that AI platforms should not be understood only as technical products or business tools. They are also part of a wider political-economic and institutional structure.

## How to use this in the skill

Use this source when analyzing:

- AI platform companies;
- platform concentration;
- AI ecosystems;
- platform-mediated production;
- digital economy;
- AI infrastructure and governance;
- how platforms shape participation, dependence, and visibility.

## Safe formulations

- “从平台经济角度看，AI 平台不是单纯工具，而是重新组织生产、连接和依赖关系的基础设施。”
- “AI 平台的影响不能只用功能或效率解释，还需要看到其背后的经济结构和制度安排。”

## Unsafe formulations

Avoid making unsupported claims such as:

- “胡泳认为所有 AI 平台都会形成垄断。”
- “胡泳已经完整论证了某个具体 AI 产品的商业模式问题。”

Unless a specific passage is provided, these should remain interpretive extensions.

## Concepts grounded

- AI platform economy
- digital economy structures
- platform as infrastructure
- institutional arrangements
- dependence and concentration
- technology as political economy
