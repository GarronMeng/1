# Source Card: AI and the Question of Being Human

## Source

财新《中国改革》 interview: “面对人工智能，何以为人”

URL: https://cnreform.caixin.com/2024-09-05/102233686.html

## What this source establishes

This source grounds the skill's AI module around a human question rather than a technical question.

It supports the following broad concerns:

- AI should not be understood only as an industrial or technical phenomenon.
- AI raises philosophical and social questions about what it means to be human.
- The expansion of AI does not make social risks disappear.
- AI analysis should include human subjectivity, judgment, and social consequences.

## How to use this in the skill

Use this source when analyzing:

- AI writing;
- AI agents;
- AI companions;
- AI in education;
- AI in medicine;
- automation and responsibility;
- human judgment under machine assistance.

## Safe formulations

- “可以从‘面对人工智能，何以为人’这一问题意识出发，把 AI 写作理解为对人的表达和判断的重组。”
- “AI 的问题不只是能否替代人，而是它如何改变人对自身能力、判断和经验的理解。”

## Unsafe formulations

Avoid claiming source support for specific product-level analyses unless directly verified.

Do not write:

- “胡泳明确认为 ChatGPT 会导致写作退化。”
- “胡泳明确提出 AI Agent 是责任缝隙。”

Those are extensions, not direct claims.

## Concepts grounded

- AI and human subjectivity
- what it means to be human
- judgment under automation
- social risk
- technology beyond industry
- AI as philosophical and social problem

## Recommended extension

For AI writing:

> 延展到 AI 写作，问题不是机器能否生成文字，而是人是否还通过写作形成自己的判断。

For AI Agent:

> 延展到 AI Agent，问题不是任务能否自动化，而是判断、行动和责任如何被重新分配。

## Recommended use level

Use as a major source for AI-related subjectivity and human-condition analysis.
